A — STYLE
16-bit pixel art, coarse 128x128 art-pixel grid enlarged to 1024x1024. Crisp square pixels, dark navy outline, flat three-tone shading, top-left light. Reference 1: project style only. Reference 2: this character's identity.

B — IDENTITY
CHARACTER: Lizz, the Medusa - a golden-scaled serpent woman (Medium monstrosity).
LOOK: human upper body with golden scaly skin, a crown of
living snakes instead of hair, glowing yellow eyes, long clawed fingers; from the hips down
a thick coiled snake tail instead of legs (the tail coil rests on the baseline; in WALK
she slithers, the tail undulates, there are no legs).
WEAPON: no weapon; she attacks with her claws, arms raised.
PALETTE (taken from the retained project portrait; use only these plus their darker/lighter tones): #98692E, #714F29, #2F2C27, #F8D95F, #DEA648, #493022, #CD953B.
ANIMATION SET: all eight rows (CAST: petrifying gaze, a yellow glow from the eyes, no projectile).


C — FORMAT
One NEW head-and-shoulders portrait 1024x1024 RGBA, three-quarter view facing viewer right. True transparent background outside silhouette, no solid fill, no checkerboard. Maximum32 opaque colours. No text, border, swatches or watermark.
